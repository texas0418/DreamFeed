Dreamfeed — Privacy Policy

Effective 24 July 2026

Dreamfeed is built to be private by default. Everything you log about your baby stays on your device. We have no servers and we never see your data.

What Dreamfeed stores, and where

Dreamfeed records feeds, nursing, sleep, diapers, your baby's name and birth date, and your app settings. All of this is stored locally on your device only, in the app's private storage. It is never uploaded to us or to anyone else. No account is required, and there is no sign-in.

Backups you create with "Export backup" are ordinary files that you control — you choose where to save or share them (Files, iCloud, email). We never receive them.

What the developer collects

Nothing. The developer of Dreamfeed does not collect, receive, store, or have any access to your baby's data. There is no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind. Dreamfeed contains no third-party tracking SDKs.

Purchases (Dreamfeed Pro)

The optional one-time "Dreamfeed Pro" purchase is handled by Apple and by RevenueCat, Inc., a service we use to unlock and restore your purchase. To do that, RevenueCat receives:

  • a randomly generated, anonymous app-user ID (not your name, email, or Apple ID);
  • your purchase and transaction details for dreamfeed_pro_lifetime; and
  • basic device, operating-system, and country information.

This is used only to validate and restore your purchase — never to identify you or track you across other apps. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy and the Apple Privacy Policy. Payment is processed by Apple; we never see your card or Apple ID.

Children's privacy

Dreamfeed is a tool for parents and caregivers to track an infant. It is not directed at children, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from children. No baby data ever leaves your device.

Deleting your data

Deleting the Dreamfeed app removes all of its on-device data permanently. For the limited purchase records held by Apple and RevenueCat, please refer to their privacy policies linked above, or contact us.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above. Material changes will be reflected here before they take effect.
