Where is my data stored?

Entirely on your device. Dreamfeed has no account and no servers — nothing is uploaded. See our Privacy Policy.

How do I back up my data?

Settings → Export backup creates a JSON file you can save to Files, iCloud, or email. To move to a new phone, export on the old one and use Restore from backup on the new one.

Will I lose my data if I delete the app?

Yes — on-device data is removed when you delete the app. Export a backup first if you want to keep it.

What does Dreamfeed Pro include?

A one-time $14.99 purchase unlocks the pediatrician PDF summary (a clean 7-day report of feeds, sleep, and diapers), plus future Pro features. Logging is always free.

I bought Pro but it's not showing / I got a new phone.

Go to Settings → Restore purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID you purchased with.

How is the "time since last feed" calculated?

From when the most recent feed started — the spacing convention pediatricians use.
