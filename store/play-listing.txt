FULL DESCRIPTION (4000 chars max)
-----------------------------------------------------
Built for the 3 am feed: a newborn tracker you can work one-handed, half-asleep, with a baby on your arm — dark by design so nobody in the room wakes up any more than they already are.

LOG IT IN TWO TAPS
• Bottles — amount and time, done before the burp
• Nursing — left, right, and how long
• Sleep — when they went down, when they surfaced
• Diapers — the record every pediatrician asks about

THE ANSWERS AT A GLANCE
"When did they last eat?" "Which side?" "How long since the nap started?" Dreamfeed keeps the answers on the home screen — no scrolling, no math, no arguing at 4 am about whose memory is right.

HISTORY THAT MAKES THE PEDIATRICIAN VISIT EASY
The full day-by-day record: feeds, amounts, sleep stretches, diaper counts. Show the chart instead of reconstructing the week from fog.

BUILT FOR EXHAUSTED HUMANS
Big targets, dark palette, zero ceremony. No account. Your baby's data lives on your phone, full stop.

You'll sleep again someday. Until then, Dreamfeed keeps the log.
